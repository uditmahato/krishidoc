Training-data attribution

The pinned training program downloads the "New Plant Diseases Dataset
(Augmented)", a derivative of PlantVillage. The current official PlantVillage
dataset card declares CC BY-SA 3.0 and requests citation of:

Mohanty, S. P., Hughes, D. P., and Salathe, M. (2016). Using deep learning for
image-based plant disease detection. Frontiers in Plant Science, 7, 1419.
https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419

Official dataset card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/mohanty/PlantVillage
Training dataset page:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vipoooool/new-plant-diseases-dataset

Important product limitation

The source notebook reports 92.44% validation accuracy on augmented,
controlled-background dataset images. KrishiDoc has not independently
reproduced that result. The model has not been calibrated or validated on
Nepal field photographs, and it has no reliable open-set/non-plant detector.
KrishiDoc therefore uses it only for experimental possible matches, never a
confirmed diagnosis or an instruction to apply a chemical treatment.

KrishiDoc potato field research model - EfficientNet-B0

Artifact identity:
90875fad84d33e06884466030e2dfe18fedc2fd89b5e71a9d61835b837891c69

This artifact was trained by the KrishiDoc pipeline from the provenance-tracked
sources and policies recorded in ml/DATASET_CARD.md, ml/datasets/sources.json,
and the immutable training manifest hash embedded in its metadata. The
EfficientNet-B0 initialization uses torchvision ImageNet-1K default weights;
torchvision is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License.

The model covers only potato early blight, potato late blight, and healthy
potato leaves. Public field evidence is dominated by India and Indonesia and
does not constitute independent Nepal validation. The app therefore presents
only experimental possible matches and applies a separate validity/OOD refusal
gate. It must not confirm a diagnosis or recommend a chemical treatment.
