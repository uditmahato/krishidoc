# Interaction and Motion Review (Module 12 pre-review)

VERDICT: zero motion code exists. A repo-wide grep for Animated/Transition/Curves/Hero/HapticFeedback/Duration( returns ONE hit: the 200ms frame interval in providers.dart:37. Three concrete holes hurt in the field: a 1.2-1.6s preparation wait covered by nothing, a coaching banner driven at 5Hz into a setState that rebuilds the whole screen including CameraPreview, and a disabled shutter that swallows taps in silence. Fixes are almost all compositor-level (opacity, colour lerp, transform), which a 2018 GPU handles free.

BUDGET ARITHMETIC that governs everything: 16.7ms/frame minus 3-5ms for QualityGate.assess on the UI isolate leaves ~11ms on every twelfth frame while the camera is live. Every capture-screen animation must be compositor-level.

## Findings (ranked)
1. **The 1.2-1.6s preparation wait is covered by nothing** (capture_screen.dart:81-93). Longest wait in the product, highest anxiety, less feedback than a dead app. Farmers will tap again. Then _preparedBytes GROWS the bottom Column, shoving the preview up: a layout jump as the reward for waiting. FIX: three-beat cover (flash, dim, linear progress, cross-fading stage label), AnimatedSize to reserve height. M / High.
2. **The coaching banner strobes at 5Hz.** 200ms is below the reading time of a short Devanagari phrase; edgeEnergy near the threshold of 8 chatters between "Ready" and "Move closer" continuously in a hand-held frame. Worse, it is COUPLED to the shutter's enabled state so the button arms/disarms at 5Hz and a tap can land in a dead window. FIX in order: (a) hysteresis on the SIGNAL: keep last 3 assessments, arm on 2-of-3, disarm only on 3-of-3, asymmetric because disarming must not yank the button from under a moving finger; (b) 900ms minimum hold, publishing message and arm-bit together from ONE value; (c) only then cross-fade 180ms + colour lerp 250ms + AnimatedSize. Make the hold a provider so tests can zero it. M / High.
3. **Tapping a disabled shutter produces literally nothing** (capture_screen.dart:148). A disabled FilledButton has no ink response and no gesture recogniser: the tap is not refused, it is UNOBSERVED. The most common failure in the app will be "farmer taps, nothing happens". FIX: keep onPressed non-null, branch inside _capture, fire heavyImpact + 300ms banner shake + return. Gate rule unchanged, only the silence. Flips four test assertions to behavioural checks on captureCount, which is the better assertion anyway. S / High.
4. **Capture/preparation failure is silent**: try/finally with no catch (capture_screen.dart:86-92). Reads identically to "the app ignored me". FIX: catch + heavyImpact + shake + a PERSISTENT inline error, never a SnackBar, because the one thing that must not evaporate is the explanation of a failure. S / High.
5. **No motion tokens in the design system.** Without them every duration drifts at the call site, which is exactly how V1 got 1.7:1 yellow-on-white. Add KdMotion (press 100, quick 180, standard 250, slow 350, coachHold 900; enter easeOutCubic, exit easeInCubic) BEFORE the first animation. Plus `kdDuration(context, d)` honouring MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf: Android "Remove animations" is disproportionately enabled on exactly this hardware. S / High (leverage).
6. **5Hz setState rebuilds the entire capture screen including CameraPreview** (:78 -> :126). This is the budget the cross-fade must be paid from. FIX: ValueNotifier<CaptureQuality?> + ValueListenableBuilder around only the banner and shutter; RepaintBoundary/const the preview. S / High (frame rate).
7. **No hero for the captured photo.** "The photo I just took became this diagnosis" is the single causal claim the product rests on; a plain push asserts nothing, a hero proves it. Same MemoryImage both sides so ImageCache dedupes; cacheWidth 720 so a 1024px JPEG never decodes to a 4MB ARGB buffer. Do NOT hero history thumbnails later. M / High.
8. **Page transitions unspecified.** Flutter 3.32's Android default ZoomPageTransitionsBuilder snapshots BOTH routes into full-screen textures (~10MB each at 1080x2340) at the first frame of every push, while a camera stream may still be alive. FIX per route: home->capture fade only 220ms (never slide a camera in; a camera does not come from anywhere, it turns on) with a black Scaffold; home->history shared-axis-X 250ms; capture->result plain fade 350ms with the hero doing the work. Pin FadeUpwardsPageTransitionsBuilder in the theme. Skip predictive back: needs Android 14, minSdk is 23. S / Med-High.
9. **History loading/empty/insert all snap.** Local drift resolves in single-digit ms, so the spinner flashes for two frames. FIX: delay any loading affordance 250ms (a load that beats 250ms should show NOTHING); past that, 4 static skeleton rows matching _HistoryTile geometry exactly; AnimatedList for genuinely new items only. Deliberately NOT shimmer: a ShaderMask gradient sweep at 60fps across a full-width list is the definition of decorative cost. M / Med.
10. **Camera preview pops in over WHITE.** 300-900ms of init shows a white rectangle then full brightness. FIX: black Scaffold, delayed 2px spinner, fade a black ColoredBox OUT over the preview; never wrap CameraPreview in Opacity (forces an offscreen buffer every frame). S / Med.
11. **Home tile ripple bleeds past rounded corners:** Card's M3 default clipBehavior is Clip.none. FIX: Clip.antiAlias + a KdRadius token. Keep elevation static and let ink carry the press: animating elevation re-rasters the shadow. S / Med.
12. **"Coming soon" tiles are indistinguishable until after the tap.** The ripple promises navigation and the snackbar retracts it 300ms later, 500px from the finger. FIX: different resting state (0.55 opacity + "Soon" chip) and different press feedback (no splash, 100ms scale to 0.98). The hand learns in one tap instead of being taught by a snackbar every time. S / Med.
13. **History rows look tappable and are inert.** Card + leading icon + two-line text is the universal signature of a tappable row. Until the detail route lands, drop the Card so it stops advertising an affordance it does not have. S / Med.
14. **Language switching has no confirmation and no current-state mark.** Add a check on the active item + selectionClick. Do NOT wrap MaterialApp in a locale-keyed AnimatedSwitcher (two full trees alive on a 1GB device for an event that happens once per install). RELATED AND WORSE: _restorePersistedLocale runs after the first frame, so a Nepali-only user sees an English home screen FLASH before it flips. Await that read before runApp; 20ms more splash beats a frame of the wrong language every cold start. S / Med.

## Choreography (capture, end to end)
Beat 0 entry 220ms fade into a BLACK scaffold, bottom bar rendered immediately so nothing reflows later. Beat 1 camera wake: black overlay, spinner only past 250ms, fade overlay out 250ms on first frame. Beat 2 coaching: 900ms hold, 2-of-3 hysteresis, 180ms cross-fade, 250ms colour lerp. Beat 3 arming (false->true edge ONLY): colour lerp + one 220ms scale pulse easeOutBack + selectionClick rate-limited to 1/1.5s. Disarming gets the colour lerp and nothing else: arming is an invitation, disarming is not an alarm. Beat 4 tap: mediumImpact SYNCHRONOUSLY before any await; white flash 60ms up / 120ms down. CRITICAL: Isolate.run spawns a fresh isolate per capture (~50-150ms + byte copy) and currently lands inside the flash's first frames, making the tap the most jank-prone point in the app. Kick preparation off from addPostFrameCallback or hold a long-lived isolate: an architecture change with a motion symptom. Beat 5 wait: own the frozen preview, dim to 55% over 200ms, LinearProgressIndicator under the app bar (a single animated rect, keeps the eye at the top edge), stage label cross-fades "Photo taken" -> "Getting it ready". A label that CHANGES proves the app is alive; a spinner proves nothing, which was V1's lesson. Beat 6 success: lightImpact, cross-fade to the prepared JPEG, hero into the result, body fades at 150ms. Beat 7 failure: heavyImpact, shake, persistent inline error, dim lifted so the live camera returns. Beat 8 return: suppress banner changes for 600ms after resume, because the farmer just came back from a result, not from nothing.

## Motion budget: what to AVOID
BackdropFilter/ImageFiltered anywhere (a full-res gaussian per frame is the most expensive thing on a 2018 GPU; the banner's solid 0.85 fill is CORRECT, never "upgrade" it to frosted glass). Opacity/FadeTransition wrapping CameraPreview. Shimmer of any kind. AnimatedSwitcher on large subtrees. Staggered list entrances. Implicit animations inside itemBuilder (re-trigger on recycle, phantom motion while scrolling). Elevation animation on press. Effect-chain packages. Flutter's default ZoomPageTransitionsBuilder. Positive obligation: gate EVERY custom duration behind MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf.

## Haptics map
flutter/services HapticFeedback, no dependency, no VIBRATE permission (routes through View.performHapticFeedback; CONTEXT_CLICK needs API 23 and minSdk IS 23). They respect the system haptic setting, which is off by default on many budget handsets, so haptics must NEVER be the only channel: every entry is paired with something visible.
- Shutter pressed, synchronously before any await: mediumImpact (without it the second tap is inevitable)
- Gate arms, false->true only, max 1/1.5s: selectionClick (the farmer is looking at the leaf, not the screen)
- Preparation completes: lightImpact
- Capture/preparation fails: heavyImpact
- Tap on a refused shutter: heavyImpact
- Crop chosen: selectionClick
- Language chosen: selectionClick
Do NOT fire on: home tile taps (InkWell already plays the system click sound), page transitions, snackbars, scrolling, or every coaching change. The arm tick is the only recurring haptic and rate-limiting it is not optional: a hand hovering at the blur threshold would turn the phone into a pager.
